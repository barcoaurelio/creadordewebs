# Gym / Fitness Template: "Ironline Strength Club"

A bold, industrial one-page site for a strength gym, CrossFit box, martial arts school, or personal training studio.

**Design:** near-black with a volt lime accent, Anton condensed display caps paired with Manrope, outlined display text, a scrolling marquee strip, and sharp rectangular cards. Zero stock-photo energy.

**Sections:** sticky nav, oversized hero with free-week CTA, marquee, four program cards, weekly class schedule table (scrolls horizontally on mobile), three-tier pricing with a highlighted plan, coach bios, join CTA, footer. Includes ExerciseGym JSON-LD structured data.

**Customize in 10 minutes:**
1. Open `index.html` in any text editor.
2. Search for "Ironline" and replace with your gym name.
3. Edit programs, the schedule table, pricing, and coach bios; search for `555` to update contact info.
4. Swap the coach-photo gradient blocks (marked `<!-- IMAGE SLOT -->`) for real photos when ready.
5. Colors and fonts live in the `tailwind.config` block near the top of the file. Change `volt` to re-theme the whole page.
