# Salon / Spa Template: "Willow & Sage"

An airy, refined one-page site for a hair salon, day spa, nail studio, esthetician, or massage practice.

**Design:** warm linen background, clay and deep pine accents, Cormorant Garamond italic display type paired with Jost, arch-shaped image frames, hairline rules, and generous whitespace. Calm on purpose.

**Sections:** sticky nav, split hero with arched photo slot, founder quote band, full service and price menu (hair, skin, spa) with hairline price leaders, dark studio section, team, three testimonials, booking CTA with hours and address, footer. Includes BeautySalon JSON-LD structured data.

**Customize in 10 minutes:**
1. Open `index.html` in any text editor.
2. Search for "Willow" and replace with your studio name.
3. Edit services and prices in the three service columns; search for `555` to update contact info.
4. Replace the arch gradient blocks (marked `<!-- IMAGE SLOT -->`) with real photos; keep the `arch` class for the shape.
5. Colors and fonts live in the `tailwind.config` block near the top. `clay` and `pine` set the whole mood.
