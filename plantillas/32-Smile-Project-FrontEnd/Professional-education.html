About Course

Short Courses

At 32 Smiles Dental College, we understand the need for continuous professional development and offer a variety of short courses tailored for dental professionals seeking to enhance their skills. These courses cover specialized topics such as cosmetic dentistry, dental implantology, orthodontics, and periodontics. Each short course is designed to provide intensive, focused training, typically lasting a few weeks to a few months, enabling practitioners to stay updated with the latest advancements in their field. Our short courses are taught by experts and are structured to include both theoretical knowledge and practical hands-on sessions. This ensures that participants can immediately apply what they learn in their dental practices, thereby improving patient care and expanding their service offerings.

Full Courses: Master's and Bachelor's Programs

Our full-course offerings at 32 Smiles Dental College include comprehensive bachelor's and master's degree programs designed to prepare students for successful careers in dentistry. The Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) program is a rigorous five-year course that combines in-depth theoretical knowledge with extensive clinical practice. Students learn about various dental disciplines, including oral surgery, prosthodontics, and pediatric dentistry, while gaining hands-on experience through supervised clinical rotations. For those looking to specialize, our Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) programs offer advanced training in fields such as endodontics, orthodontics, and periodontology. The MDS programs are research-oriented, allowing students to contribute to the advancement of dental science through innovative research projects. Both programs emphasize the development of clinical skills, critical thinking, and ethical practice, ensuring graduates are well-equipped to meet the demands of the dental profession.

About Our College

Library

Our library at 32 Smiles Dental College is one of the largest and most comprehensive dental libraries in the region. It houses an extensive collection of books, journals, research papers, and digital resources covering all aspects of dentistry. The library is equipped with comfortable reading areas, computer terminals, and private study rooms, providing an ideal environment for research and learning. We continually update our collection to ensure that students and faculty have access to the latest information and developments in the field of dentistry. The library staff are also available to assist with research queries and provide guidance on accessing various resources. In addition to our extensive physical and digital collections, our library offers several innovative services to enhance the learning experience. We provide access to numerous online databases, enabling students and faculty to easily find relevant literature and stay current with emerging trends and research findings. Our interlibrary loan service allows users to borrow materials from other libraries, further expanding the range of available resources. We also host regular workshops and training sessions on topics such as literature search techniques, reference management, and academic writing skills. These sessions are designed to equip students with the essential skills needed for effective research and academic success. Our knowledgeable library staff are always on hand to provide personalized support, whether it’s helping to locate specific materials or offering advice on research methodologies. The library also features group study rooms that can be reserved for collaborative projects, fostering teamwork and peer learning. Additionally, our quiet zones are perfect for individual study and concentration. The modern design and comfortable seating options make the library a welcoming space for all users.

Case Studies

Case studies are an integral part of the curriculum at 32 Smiles Dental College, providing students with valuable real-world insights into various dental conditions and treatment approaches. Through detailed analysis of actual patient cases, students learn to diagnose, plan, and execute effective treatment strategies. These case studies cover a wide range of dental issues, from common problems like cavities and gum disease to complex conditions such as maxillofacial anomalies and dental trauma. By engaging with case studies, students develop critical thinking skills and gain practical experience, preparing them for clinical practice.
