Our Services

Specialty Services

Specialty dental services address specific and complex dental conditions. Temporomandibular joint disorder (TMD) therapy relieves jaw pain and dysfunction, while sleep apnea treatment helps manage breathing issues during sleep. Cleft lip and palate care provides comprehensive treatment for congenital oral deformities, improving function and appearance.

Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (TMD) Therapy

TMD therapy at 32 Smiles Dental Care treats disorders of the temporomandibular joint, relieving pain and improving function. Our customized treatments address the underlying causes of TMD for lasting relief.

Sleep Apnea Treatment

Sleep apnea treatment at 32 Smiles Dental Care includes custom oral appliances that help keep your airway open during sleep. These appliances improve breathing, reduce snoring, and enhance sleep quality.

Cleft Lip and Palate Care

Preventive Services

Dental Cleanings

(Prophylaxis)

At 32 Smiles Dental Care, regular dental cleanings (prophylaxis) are essential for maintaining oral hygiene and preventing dental issues. Our skilled hygienists remove plaque and tartar buildup, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease, and leaving your teeth feeling fresh and clean.

Dental Exams

Dental exams at 32 Smiles Dental Care are comprehensive evaluations of your oral health. Our dentists check for signs of decay, gum disease, and other dental problems, ensuring any issues are detected and treated early to maintain optimal oral health.

Book An Appointment


Digital X-Rays

32 Smiles Dental Care uses advanced digital X-rays to obtain detailed images of your teeth and jaw. These X-rays help us accurately diagnose issues that are not visible during a regular exam, allowing for precise and effective treatment planning.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments at 32 Smiles Dental Care strengthen your tooth enamel, making it more resistant to decay. This quick and painless procedure is especially beneficial for children and patients prone to cavities, providing long-lasting protection.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants at 32 Smiles Dental Care are a preventive measure to protect your molars from decay. The sealants are applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth, creating a barrier that prevents food and bacteria from getting trapped in the grooves.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Oral cancer screenings are a vital part of our preventive services at 32 Smiles Dental Care. Our dentists perform thorough examinations to detect any early signs of oral cancer, ensuring prompt treatment and better outcomes.

Restorative Services

Fillings

(Composite, Amalgam)

At 32 Smiles Dental Care, we offer both composite and amalgam fillings to restore teeth affected by cavities. Composite fillings match your tooth color for a natural look, while amalgam fillings provide durable, long-lasting results.

Crowns (Caps)

Bridges

Dental Implants

Dentures

(Complete and Partial)

32 Smiles Dental Care provides both complete and partial dentures to restore your smile and improve your ability to chew and speak. Our dentures are custom-fitted for comfort and designed to look natural.

Inlays and onlays at 32 Smiles Dental Care are custom-made restorations used to repair teeth with moderate damage or decay. They provide a stronger and more durable solution than traditional fillings, preserving more of your natural tooth structure.

Endodontic Services

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy at 32 Smiles Dental Care treats infected or damaged tooth pulp, relieving pain and saving the tooth. This procedure cleans and seals the tooth’s interior, preventing further infection and restoring function.

Book An Appointment


Apicoectomy

Apicoectomy at 32 Smiles Dental Care is a surgical procedure that removes the tip of a tooth’s root to treat persistent infection. This procedure is often performed when root canal therapy alone is not sufficient to resolve the issue.

Book An Appointment


Pulpotomy

Pulpotomy at 32 Smiles Dental Care is a procedure commonly used in pediatric dentistry to remove infected pulp from the crown of a baby tooth. This treatment preserves the tooth and prevents further infection.

Book An Appointment


Pulpectomy

Pulpectomy at 32 Smiles Dental Care involves removing infected pulp from both the crown and roots of a tooth. This procedure is typically performed on primary teeth to save the tooth and maintain proper spacing for permanent teeth.

Book An Appointment
